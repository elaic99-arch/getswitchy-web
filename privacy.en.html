Privacy policy

Your privacy,
in plain language.

This policy explains what personal data is collected when you use the Switchy app and this website, why it is collected, who it is shared with and what rights you have. It is written to comply with the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, 5741-1981.

Last updated: September 19, 2026 · We may update this policy from time to time.

The controller of the database and the party responsible for it is Astra Tech Investments Ltd (company number 517351896), of Boker 2, Herzliya, Israel.

2. What data is collected

  • Registration details.
  • Contact details.
  • Delivery address.
  • Listing content and photographs you upload.
  • In-app messages.
  • Transaction history.
  • Device and usage data.

3. Why it is collected

  • Operating the service.
  • Processing orders and payments.
  • Delivery.
  • Support and dispute handling.
  • Fraud prevention.
  • Legal compliance.
  • Service improvement.
  • Marketing, where you have consented.

4. Who data is shared with

We share data only to the extent needed for the purposes above, and with:

  • The delivery company (Zigzag) — to carry out the delivery.
  • The authentication provider — when authentication was requested for an item.
  • The payment services provider — to process the payment and any refund.
  • Hosting, infrastructure and analytics providers — to run and maintain the service.
  • Authorities — where required by law.

We do not sell personal data.

5. Data retention

We keep personal data for as long as the account is active, and after that for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for or to meet legal obligations — for example accounting records or an open dispute.

Data that is no longer needed is deleted or kept in a form that does not identify you.

6. Security

We take accepted security measures to protect the data, including encryption in transit, limiting access permissions to staff who need them for their role, and monitoring for unusual access.

No system is completely immune, and we work continuously to reduce risk.

7. Your rights

Under Israeli law you may review your personal data, ask to correct data that is incorrect, incomplete or inaccurate, and ask to delete it.

You can exercise these rights directly in the app — by editing your profile details or deleting your account — or by contacting us at support@getswitchy.com. We reply within the period set by law.

8. Cookies and data on this website

This website introduces Switchy. It does not include advertising trackers or analytics tools. Fonts and images are hosted with the website; fonts are not loaded from Google Fonts.

The selected language appears in the page address. It is not saved in your browser’s localStorage.

The contact form prepares a message in your browser. It does not automatically send the information you enter to us. You can review the message, then open and send it from your own email app; we receive the information when you choose to send it.

Private access and the editor use ChatGPT sign-in. The editor is restricted to the owner. Saved changes, their date and the account identifier of the editor are stored on the server to maintain the website content.

9. Marketing communications

We send marketing messages only if you have consented. Every such message includes an unsubscribe option, and you can also ask to be removed at any time by writing to support@getswitchy.com.

Operational messages about your orders and your account are sent regardless of marketing consent.

10. Age

The service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data about anyone younger.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy from time to time. We will give notice of a material change in the app or by email, and the date at the top of this page will be updated.
