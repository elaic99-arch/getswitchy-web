The essentials for discovering, buying and selling on Switchy.

Contact us
01What is Switchy?+

A social marketplace for new and pre-loved fashion. Discover looks, follow profiles, buy, sell and create your own universe.

02Who can create a shop?+

Individuals, creators and shops can share their pieces and develop their own world on Switchy.

03New or second-hand?+

Both. Each listing describes the condition of the piece to help you choose.

04Buy now or Make an offer?+

Buy now lets you proceed at the listed price. Make an offer lets you propose a price to the seller. Check the details before confirming in the app.

05How does shipping work?+

Options may include home delivery with Zigzag, a collection point or pickup from the seller. Check your order’s options and the shipping policy.

06Can I authenticate an item?+

Switchy offers optional authenticity checks for eligible items. Conditions are explained during checkout.

Authentication
07An issue with an order?+

Contact support in the app or write to support@getswitchy.com. Applicable terms are set out in the shipping and returns policies.

08Can I buy on this website?+

This site introduces Switchy and its universe. Purchases, offers and sales take place in the app. Contact the Switchy team if you need help.

09How can I delete my Switchy account?+

In the app: Settings → General settings → Delete my account.

If you can’t access the app, email support@getswitchy.com from the address on your account.
